The team behind it

Who are we?

The La Farina project was born from the volunteer work of Chef Pollyanna Serrano during her time in Timor-Leste, together with Aroma Café's investment in facilities and equipment, in order to train young Timorese people in vulnerable situations in baking, fresh pasta, pastry and many other culinary skills. La Farina offers high-quality products inspired by Italian recipes and techniques, made by hand by young women.

For now, while La Farina does not have a physical space, we continue to support the girls through orders on WhatsApp and home delivery — every product sold helps build a new future for young Timorese people, thanks to the support of the community.
