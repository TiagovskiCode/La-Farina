Our Story

Flavours with a purpose

From our kitchen to your table — meet the people behind La Farina.

How it all began

One kitchen, two students and a fresh start

The La Farina project was born from the volunteer work of Chef Pollyanna Serrano in Dili, training young Timorese people in vulnerable situations, with intensive daily mentoring for Nélia and Verônica, sharing techniques in baking, fresh pasta and pastry.

For now, while we look for a physical space, we continue to support the girls through orders on WhatsApp and home delivery — and every product sold helps build a new future for young Timorese people through the power of community and good food.

Vision

To be a doorway of opportunity for young Timorese women through skills, work and growth — a reference in artisan baking and pasta in Timor-Leste.

Mission

To combine Italian techniques with local ingredients, offering quality artisan products and creating job opportunities for young Timorese people, especially women.

Values

Quality, integrity, empowerment, sustainability and hospitality — in every supplier we choose and in every product that leaves our kitchen.

“I never imagined I could make recipes as different as focaccia, cinnamon rolls and many others, and also learn how important it is to understand the techniques and the type of flour for each product, and the resting and baking times.”
— Nélia

Who teaches

The chefs behind La Farina

Helder Machado

Management & Training

Brazilian chef with more than 7 years of experience in Timor-Leste. He is general manager and head chef of Aroma Café — voted Best Restaurant in Timor-Leste in 2022 by USAID's Tourism for All initiative — where he also leads the culinary training of young Timorese people.

Pollyanna Serrano

Italian Gastronomy

Brazilian chef specialising in fresh pasta and Italian artisan baking. She trained the girls of the project directly, sharing her techniques and experience in the La Farina kitchen.
