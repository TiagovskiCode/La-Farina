Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026. Here we explain, in plain language, what happens to your data when you visit the La Farina website.

The essentials, in short

La Farina has no server of its own and no customer database. The site is a simple storefront and shop: your shopping cart is stored only on your own phone or computer, and the order is completed through a WhatsApp message that you send yourself. We do not sell or share data with third parties for advertising purposes.

What is stored on your device

When you add products to the cart, this information (which products and how many) is stored locally in your browser — known as localStorage — so your cart is not lost if you leave the site and come back. This information never leaves your device, is not sent to any La Farina server, and disappears if you clear your browser data.

WhatsApp and Instagram

When you complete an order, leave feedback or contact us through WhatsApp or Instagram, that conversation takes place inside those platforms, which belong to Meta. From that moment on, the handling of your data (name, phone number, messages) follows the privacy policies of WhatsApp and Instagram, not ours. We recommend that you read those policies if you have questions about how those companies use information.

Analytics

This site does not yet use any visitor statistics tool. If that changes in the future (for example, to understand how many people visit the shop), this page will be updated to explain it before the tool goes live.

Cookies

The site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The only data stored on your device is what is described above (the shopping cart), through localStorage, which is technically not a cookie and is not shared with anyone.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data in our own systems, there is no "account" or "profile" for you to ask us to delete. If at any point you sent us data through WhatsApp or Instagram (for example, your name and address for a delivery) and you would like us to delete it from our conversations, simply ask us directly through that channel.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page as the site grows (for example, by adding analytics or a more advanced ordering system). We will always show the date of the last update at the top of the page.

Contact

For any questions about this policy or your data, get in touch with us through WhatsApp or Instagram — the contact details are in the footer of this page.
